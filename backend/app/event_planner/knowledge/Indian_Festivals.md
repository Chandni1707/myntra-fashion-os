This fashion guide applies to most Indian festivals including:

Diwali,
Holi,
Chhath Puja,
Navratri,
Durga Puja,
Ganesh Chaturthi,
Janmashtami,
Raksha Bandhan,
Karva Chauth,
Pongal,
Onam,
Ugadi,
Makar Sankranti,
Baisakhi,
Lohri,
Vishu,
Gudi Padwa,
Eid,
Christmas,
Guru Nanak Jayanti.

## Occasion

Indian festivals are cultural and religious celebrations attended with family and friends.

## Dress Code

Traditional ethnic clothing is generally preferred.

Women commonly wear:
- Saree
- Lehenga
- Salwar Suit
- Kurti Set
- Anarkali

Men commonly wear:
- Kurta Pajama
- Kurta with Jeans
- Nehru Jacket
- Sherwani (special occasions)
- Dhoti (regional festivals)
